# About Us

Our journey began nearly 20 years ago with a search for a domain name that referenced a living entity and evoked a sense of self.

In the early days, it was a small server running at home. Then, it evolved into a file-based template system. Today, it continues as an access point where we present the software and technology solutions we develop.

With our team of highly qualified software engineers, we provide end-to-end solutions and support services, from accurately analyzing needs and creating appropriate system architectures to software development and improving existing systems.

We approach each project with patterns suitable for its specific needs and conditions. Our goal is not just to deliver working software, but to create understandable, sustainable, and scalable systems over time.
